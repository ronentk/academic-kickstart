title: "Participatory Science"
event: Panel at Science Summit NYC 2026

summary: "Panel on participatory research across the full research lifecycle — priorities, methods, data collection, sensemaking, dissemination, and policy."
abstract: "Science has always been pursued by the curious, and scientists have come together in community to make strides that they could not have achieved on their own. Participatory science is the exemplar of this outlook, and presents a model for addressing pressing global challenges. Participatory scientific research spans the entire research lifecycle - defining what research to pursue based on community needs, values, and priorities, exploration of methods and hypotheses generated by unique perspective, representative data collection and surfacing of local knowledge, analysis harnessing diverse human insights and intuition, all the way through sharing outcomes, collective sensemaking, and a participatory shaping of future policy. This session explores how enabling broad, deep engagement in research leads to increased agency, and increased agency leads to support, trust, robustness, and effectiveness in the research enterprise to address global challenges from health, to sustainability, to equitable prosperity and beyond. A rotating panel of experts will work through topics including: what participatory science is and why it matters; how the public can be invited to participate throughout the research lifecycle; the synergy between participatory research and open science; barriers to agency in the research ecosystem; and how to create regenerative, sustainable development through participatory research. This session was developed in partnership between Frameshifter and the Association for Advancing Participatory Sciences."
